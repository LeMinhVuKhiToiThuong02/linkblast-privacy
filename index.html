Privacy Policy — Link Blast

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Link Blast (the "App", package com.gradientcat.linkblast), published by GradientCat ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • No account creation, sign-in, personal name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location are requested or collected.
  • Your core game progress, unlocked skins, and settings are saved locally on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app performance, usage trends, and to fix application crashes.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not display third-party advertisements within the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Level progress, unlocked skins, daily puzzle states, and personal settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers. It is permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data via device settings.

3. Information Collected Automatically

To help us improve the gaming experience, the App uses third-party services provided by Google LLC. These services collect the following information automatically:

Firebase Analytics collects standard usage statistics such as app opens, sessions, engagement time, and application updates, alongside:

  • An automatically generated app instance identifier and the device Advertising ID (if available and allowed by your system permissions);
  • Device specifications (model, operating system version, language settings, screen size) and App version;
  • An approximate country/region derived from the device's IP address.

Firebase Crashlytics generates technical reports when the App encounters a crash or unexpected critical error. This includes: stack traces, device model, OS version, App version, memory/disk state at the time of the incident, and a unique Crashlytics installation identifier.

We use this data solely to measure application usage, optimize gameplay, and resolve technical bugs. It is never used to identify you personally.

For more details, please review Google's official documentation: Firebase Privacy and Security and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing and Processing

Information is processed securely by Google LLC acting as our service provider for Firebase. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any collected data with other third parties, except where strictly required to comply with judicial or law enforcement obligations.

5. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics retains user-level and event-level data for up to 14 months. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash reports for up to 90 days before automated deletion. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify any device may be kept for longer periods.

6. Your Privacy Choices & Control

  • You can reset or delete your device's Advertising ID at any time (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App from your device, which also wipes all local save data.
  • You may contact us via email to request deletion of data associated with your app installation; please provide your device model and approximate times of usage to help us locate the relevant records.

7. Data Security

Data transmitted to Firebase services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS. While no digital system can guarantee complete security, we rely on the infrastructure protections provided by Google to keep this data safe.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App. Any updates will be published on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes agreement with the revised policy.
