Privacy Policy — Link Blast

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how Link Blast (the "App", package com.gradientcat.linkblast), published by GradientCat ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • No account creation, sign-in, name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location are requested or collected.
  • Your game progress, unlocked skins, and settings are saved locally on your device.
  • The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to deliver and measure ads.
  • We use Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) to understand usage, fix crashes, and adjust App settings remotely.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Level progress, unlocked skins, daily puzzle states, free hints, and settings (sound, music, vibration) are saved locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers. It is permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data via device settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads: banners, full-screen ads (for example between levels and when the App opens), and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for an in-game reward such as a hint. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which may collect and use the following to serve, personalize, limit the frequency of, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud:

  • The device Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), if available and allowed by your device settings;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and App information (model, operating system version, language, App version);
  • Interactions with ads (ads shown, clicked, or watched to the end).

Depending on your region and choices, the ads you see may be personalized based on your interests, or non-personalized (based only on general context such as the App and your approximate location). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's AdMob privacy information.

Ad partners (mediation)

To show ads, Google AdMob may request ads from other advertising networks ("mediation partners"). Each partner that receives an ad request may collect the same kinds of information listed above, under its own privacy policy. The partners currently used by the App are:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — Privacy Policy

When partners are added or removed, this list is updated and the effective date of this policy changes. Players asked for consent (see below) can review every partner in the consent form.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and other regions where required)

Where the law requires it, the App asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent management platform (User Messaging Platform, certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). The form lists the purposes and the advertising partners concerned. Your choice is also passed to Google services such as Firebase Analytics through Google Consent Mode, which limits data storage and ad personalization when you do not consent. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options in the App.

4. Analytics, Crash Reports, and Remote Settings (Google Firebase)

Firebase Analytics collects usage statistics such as app opens, sessions, engagement time, and App updates, along with:

  • An automatically generated app instance identifier and the device Advertising ID (if available and allowed by your device settings);
  • Device specifications (model, operating system version, language, screen size) and App version;
  • An approximate country/region derived from the device's IP address.

Firebase Crashlytics generates technical reports when the App crashes or hits a critical error: stack traces, device model, OS version, App version, memory/disk state at the time of the incident, and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

Firebase Remote Config lets us change App settings (such as ad frequency and the number of free hints) without an update. To deliver these settings it uses a Firebase installation identifier, the App version, and basic device information.

We use this data only to measure usage, improve gameplay, configure the App, and fix technical problems. We do not use it to identify you personally. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

5. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases (for example removing ads or unlocking content). Purchases are processed by Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing. We never receive your payment card or billing details; we only receive the information needed to confirm, deliver, and restore a purchase (such as the product purchased, an order ID, and a purchase token). This information is used only to give you what you bought and to prevent fraud. Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Data Sharing

The information described above is collected and processed by Google LLC, acting as the provider of AdMob, Firebase, and Google Play services, under the Google Privacy Policy, and by the ad partners listed in section 3 under their own policies. Advertising data may be shared with advertising partners to deliver and measure ads. We do not sell, rent, or trade your data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where strictly required to comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics retains user-level and event-level data for up to 14 months. Firebase Crashlytics retains crash reports for up to 90 days. Advertising data is retained by Google and the ad partners according to their own policies. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for payments and tax law. Aggregated statistics that cannot identify any device may be kept longer.

8. Your Privacy Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads, or reset or delete your Advertising ID, at any time (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you were asked for ad consent, change or withdraw it at any time from Settings → Privacy Options in the App.
  • Stop all data collection by uninstalling the App, which also deletes all locally saved data.
  • Contact us by email to request deletion of data associated with your installation; please include your device model and approximate times of use so we can locate the records.

9. Data Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. While no system can guarantee complete security, we rely on the protections provided by Google's infrastructure to keep this data safe.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App. Updates will be published on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
